# ADTIS administrator guide

For the ADTIS organising team. Everything here is done in the TRADE app, under **ADTIS** in the top navigation
(`/summit/commerce`), signed in with an Entrust **administrator** or **facilitator** account. You never need a developer
to change a price, a benefit, a booth number or a payment instruction.

| Who | Can do |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Everything: settings and prices, and all the work below |
| Facilitator | The day-to-day work (applications, enquiries, payments, delegates, check-in, the directory, reports). Can look at settings but not change them, and can't change an amount owed |

## 1. Before the website sells anything: set up the edition

1. Open **Settings**. If no edition exists, press **Create the edition** to start ADTIS 2027 from the standard catalogue (the
   five exhibition packages, six sponsorship levels, five delegate categories and seven additional opportunities, at the prices
   in the website instructions). The first edition becomes the one the website sells.
2. **Edition settings**
   - *Dates*: shown as words on the website. Keep "January 2027 (exact dates to be announced)" until the days are fixed. The
     site never shows a date you haven't typed.
   - *Do the prices include taxes?* Leave **Not confirmed yet** until the team has decided about VAT, and edit the *tax note*
     so it says what is true. The note is shown under every price and in emails.
   - *Payment methods*: add at least one (name and instructions: account name, number, bank, the reference to quote, or a
     payment link). These words go in the approval emails. **Until you add one, approved exhibitors and sponsors are told the
     team will send instructions.** Nothing is made up.
   - *Exhibition zones*: one per line (for example Hall A). Exhibitors can ask for one; you give each booking one.
   - *Assessment period for free SME places*: choose the SME assessment period. Its selected SMEs register free and get
     directory profiles.
3. **Packages, levels, categories and opportunities.** Press **Edit** on any line to change its name, short description, price,
   benefits, number available, status and order.
   - **Number available** is how many booths or passes you have. Leave it empty until you know it: with no number, nothing
     sells out. Once set, the website stops taking applications for a package when it is full, and shows "Sold out".
   - **Status**: *Draft* is hidden, *Published* is on the website, *Archived* is kept for the records that name it but hidden.
     Nothing is ever deleted.
   - **Sponsorship benefits** are text for each area (logo, speaking, exhibition space, delegate passes, website and digital,
     social media, B2B matchmaking, networking, category exclusivity). Each level has its own text; an empty area means the
     level doesn't include it. **The text that comes with the standard catalogue is a proposal: read and edit every level before
     you tell sponsors about them.**
   - Changing a price changes it from now on. Bookings already made keep the price they were made at.
4. **Starting ADTIS 2028**: in Settings, *Start a new edition* and choose *a copy of* the current one. Prices, benefits and
   settings come across; bookings and registrations don't.

## 2. Exhibitors

**Exhibitors** lists every application. A booth is held only when you approve, and confirmed only when the payment is verified.

1. Open an application. Read it. **Approve application** (with an optional message to the applicant) or **Reject** (with a
   reason, which the applicant is emailed).
2. Approving holds one booth of that package, opens a payment for the price the applicant applied at, and emails them the payment
   instructions. It is refused if the package has no booth left.
3. Give the booth a **booth number** and **zone** and press **Save booth**. A number belongs to one booking.
4. When the money arrives, record it under **Payment** (see section 5).
5. **Confirm booth** becomes available once the application is approved, the payment is verified and there is a booth number. It
   emails the exhibitor their booking confirmation. The page lists what is still missing.
6. **Cancel booking** releases the booth. An unpaid payment is cancelled with it; a verified payment stays so you can refund it.

## 3. Sponsors

**Sponsors** lists sponsorship and opportunity enquiries (pavilions, sessions, directory advertisement, reception).

- Move an enquiry along the pipeline with the buttons: *New enquiry, Contacted, Negotiation, Proposal sent, Confirmed,
  Declined*. Add a note to a move; the history keeps who moved it and when.
- Set the **value** if the deal is negotiated (it starts as the listed price) and keep private **notes**.
- **Confirmed** makes the organisation a sponsor: it opens a payment for the value, emails the payment instructions and counts
  against the level's number available. For category exclusivity, set the Title Partner's number available to 1: a second
  confirmation is then refused. Benefits are confirmed in a signed sponsorship agreement, which this system doesn't replace.

## 4. Delegates and check-in

**Delegates** lists everyone registered, with their payment. A paid delegate's **badge is valid only after you verify their
payment**; they are emailed when you do.

- *Selected SMEs* register themselves free with their application number and the email of their Entrust account. Each business
  has one free place (change it in Settings). Their colleagues register as General Business Delegates.
- Correct a misspelt name before printing (**Save details**), **Resend confirmation** if someone lost their email, or **Cancel
  registration** (not possible after check-in).
- **Check-in** (the tab): scan the QR code on the badge, or type the reference (DEL-00042), and press Find. A scanner that types
  like a keyboard works. The screen shows **Valid badge**, **Already checked in** or **Do not admit yet** with the reason.
  Check in the valid ones. There is no override: to admit someone who paid in cash, record and verify their payment first.

## 5. Payments

Every booth, sponsorship and delegate registration has one payment, shown on its page.

- Record the **reference** the payer gave, the **method**, the **amount received** and attach the **proof** (a PDF, JPG or PNG).
  Recording these moves the payment to *Awaiting verification*.
- **Verify and confirm payment** marks it paid. It needs a reference or proof, and an amount received that covers the amount
  due. **Nothing else in the system ever marks anything paid.**
- Only an administrator changes the *amount due*. A confirmed payment can be marked *Refunded*; a never-paid one can be
  cancelled and reopened.
- Statuses: Pending, Awaiting verification, Confirmed, Cancelled, Refunded.

## 6. The SME directory

**SME directory** holds a public page for each selected SME.

1. After the committee's final list is approved, press **Make drafts for N selected SMEs**. Each draft is built from what the
   business said about itself. Scores, findings, finances and the contact's email and telephone are never copied.
2. Open a profile, edit the text, and show it to the business. When they agree, record **how** they approved it ("approved by Amina
   by email on 12 January") and **Publish on the site**.
3. If you change the text of an approved profile, the business's approval is cleared and the page comes off the site, because they
   approved different words. Use **Save as a small correction** for a typo.
4. Visitors reach a business through **Request B2B Matchmaking**. The business's owners get the message by email; you see every
   enquiry under *B2B enquiries* and mark it followed up.

## 7. Dashboard and reports

- **Dashboard**: registrations, booths, sponsorship and revenue, added up live. *Expected* is what is committed, *Received* is only
  what you have verified, *Outstanding* is the difference, and *Potential* (applications and enquiries still open) is separate.
  If some package has no number of booths, the totals say they are partial.
- **Reports**: delegates, exhibitors, sponsorship, payments, revenue, and countries and sectors, each as Excel, CSV or PDF. They hold
  personal details: keep the files safe. Every download is logged.

## 8. The website

The public pages (`/exhibit`, `/sponsor`, `/participate`, `/smes`, and the information pages) read the edition live, so a change in
Settings is on the site at once. The speakers, partners and news lists are in `apps/summit/src/lib/info.ts` for now: a developer
adds a confirmed name there. **Never list a speaker, sponsor or partner before the organisers have confirmed them.**

## Things the system will not do on its own

- Confirm a booth before you approve it and verify its payment.
- Mark any payment paid.
- Show an assessment score, a finding or a financial record to the public.
- Publish an SME profile the business hasn't approved.
- Sell more booths or places than the number available.
